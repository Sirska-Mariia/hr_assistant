# Remote Work Policy — Nordlight Software
Version 2.0 · Owner: HR and Security

## 1. Default model
1.1 Nordlight is hybrid: employees are expected in the office 2 days per week unless their contract says fully remote.
1.2 Office days are agreed with the team lead, who must ensure at least one overlapping day for the team.

## 2. Fully remote work
2.1 Fully remote work requires written approval from the manager and HR.
2.2 Fully remote employees are expected to visit the office for team events, at least twice a year.

## 3. Working from abroad
3.1 Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year.
3.2 Requests must be submitted at least 10 working days in advance and approved by the manager and Security.
3.3 Working from abroad is not allowed during probation.
3.4 The employee must be available within 3 hours of the Kyiv time zone and must use the company VPN.
3.5 Tax and legal implications of working abroad for longer periods are not covered by this policy.

## 4. Security
4.1 Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and the company VPN when working outside the office.
4.2 Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN enabled.
