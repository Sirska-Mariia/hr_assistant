# Benefits Policy — Nordlight Software
Version 3.0 · Owner: HR

## 1. Eligibility
1.1 Benefits in this policy apply to full-time employees. Contractors are not eligible unless their agreement says otherwise.
1.2 Unless stated otherwise, benefits start after the 3-month probation period.

## 2. Health insurance
2.1 The company pays 100% of the basic private health insurance plan for the employee.
2.2 Family coverage is available at the employee's own cost through the insurer.

## 3. Learning budget
3.1 800 USD per calendar year for courses, books and conferences, after probation.
3.2 Expenses must be approved by the manager before purchase and submitted with receipts within 30 days.
3.3 Unused learning budget does not carry over.

## 4. Wellness
4.1 30 USD per month for a gym or sports subscription, reimbursed against receipts, after probation.

## 5. Home office equipment
5.1 A one-time allowance of 300 USD for a chair, monitor or desk, available to employees working remotely at least 3 days per week, after probation.
