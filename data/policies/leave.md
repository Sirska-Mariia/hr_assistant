# Leave Policy — Nordlight Software
Version 2.1 · Owner: HR

## 1. Scope
1.1 This policy applies to all full-time employees.
1.2 Contractors are not covered. Contractor time off is agreed directly with the project lead and governed by the contractor agreement.

## 2. Leave types and yearly allowances
2.1 Annual leave: 20 working days per calendar year.
2.2 Sick leave: 10 paid working days per calendar year (details in the Sick Leave Policy).
2.3 Unpaid leave: up to 10 working days per calendar year.
2.4 Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding company holidays (1 January, 7 January, 25 December).
2.5 Employees who join mid-year receive a pro-rated allowance, set by HR at onboarding.

## 3. Eligibility
3.1 The probation period is the first 3 months from the start date.
3.2 During probation, annual leave cannot be taken. Sick leave and unpaid leave remain available.

## 4. Notice and approval
4.1 Annual leave and unpaid leave must be requested at least 5 working days before the first day of leave. Sick leave has no notice period and may be recorded up to 3 days after it started.
4.2 Notice is counted in working days from the day of submission (inclusive) to the day before the first day of leave.
4.3 Requests that meet all rules in this policy are approved automatically; requests that break any rule are declined with the reason.
4.4 Exceptions (shorter notice, longer duration) are not processed automatically. Contact HR, who will consult your manager.
4.5 Managers and HR staff follow the same rules as every other employee. A management or HR role does not grant exemptions, and nobody can approve their own exception.

## 5. Limits
5.1 A single annual leave request may not exceed 10 working days.
5.2 Annual leave cannot be taken during blackout periods: the last 7 days of March, June and September, and 21–31 December.
5.3 A request may not overlap with your own approved leave.
5.4 A request is declined if the remaining balance for that leave type is insufficient.

## 6. Carry-over
6.1 Up to 5 unused annual leave days carry over until 31 March of the following year; the rest expire.
6.2 Sick and unpaid leave do not carry over.

## 7. Cancellation
7.1 An approved request can be cancelled before its first day by contacting HR.
