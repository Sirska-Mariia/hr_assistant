# Sick Leave Policy — Nordlight Software
Version 1.4 · Owner: HR

## 1. Entitlement
1.1 Full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, from their first day of employment.
1.2 Contractors are not entitled to paid sick leave.

## 2. Reporting
2.1 Notify your manager by 10:00 on the first day of absence (chat or phone).
2.2 Record the absence in the HR system within 3 days of its start.

## 3. Medical certificate
3.1 No certificate is needed for absences of up to 3 consecutive working days.
3.2 From the 4th consecutive working day, a medical certificate must be sent to HR within 5 working days of returning.
3.3 Certificates are stored by HR only and are not shared with managers.

## 4. Longer absences
4.1 Absences longer than 10 working days in a year, or any planned medical procedure, are handled individually by HR. Contact HR before making arrangements.

## 5. Sick during annual leave
5.1 If you fall ill during approved annual leave and provide a certificate, the sick days can be re-booked as sick leave. Contact HR to adjust balances.
