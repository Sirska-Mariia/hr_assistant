# Onboarding Policy — Nordlight Software
Version 1.2 · Owner: HR and People Operations

## 1. Before day one
1.1 IT ships a laptop to the employee's address at least 3 working days before the start date.
1.2 HR sends the contract pack and the first-week schedule one week before the start date.

## 2. First week
2.1 Day 1: welcome meeting with HR, access to email, chat and the ticket portal.
2.2 Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy for the first 3 months.
2.3 Mandatory security awareness training must be completed within 14 days of the start date.

## 3. Probation
3.1 Probation lasts 3 months from the start date.
3.2 Review meetings with the manager take place at day 30, day 60 and day 90.
3.3 Probation can be extended by up to 1 month only with written agreement of HR and the employee.

## 4. Benefits during onboarding
4.1 Health insurance starts after probation (see Benefits Policy).
4.2 Employees may use up to 200 USD of the learning budget during probation, but only for courses marked mandatory by their manager.

## 5. Documents
5.1 Signed contract, tax form and emergency contact must be returned by the end of week 1.
