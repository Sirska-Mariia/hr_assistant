# Internal Requests Policy — Nordlight Software
Version 1.1 · Owner: IT and Finance

## 1. How to submit
1.1 All internal requests are submitted through the ticket portal. Requests sent by chat or email are not tracked.

## 2. IT equipment
2.1 Standard laptops are replaced every 3 years.
2.2 Replacement of broken equipment is handled within 5 working days; urgent requests that block work within 2 working days.
2.3 Additional monitors and peripherals up to 150 USD are approved by the team lead; above 150 USD they need approval from the department head.

## 3. Access requests
3.1 Access to systems is granted on a least-privilege basis and requires the approval of the system owner.
3.2 Production access requires completed security training and manager approval, and is reviewed every 6 months.

## 4. Expense reimbursement
4.1 Expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of submitting receipts in the ticket portal.
4.2 Expenses without receipts are not reimbursed.

## 5. Employment documents
5.1 Employment certificates and income letters are issued by HR within 3 working days of the request.
5.2 Changes of name, address or bank details must be reported to HR with supporting documents.
